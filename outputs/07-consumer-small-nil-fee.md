## Where you stand — ₹24,000

### ⏳ Your deadline

🟢 **You have about 674 days left.**

You have **2 years** from **10 September 2026** (the date the problem arose or the refund was refused). That runs out on **10 September 2028**.

### 🏛️ Where to go

**Try these first — they are free and often enough:**

- **National Consumer Helpline (1915)** — Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in. They take it up with the company directly.
  - Usually takes: days to a few weeks

**If that does not work:**

- **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission** — fee nil; 3 months is the statutory target; in practice often longer; you can do this yourself
  - File online yourself at edaakhil.nic.in, or in person at the District Commission for the district where you live or work.

### 📄 What to gather

**You already have:**

- ✅ proof that you paid (bank entry, receipt or bill)

Anything in writing where they admit the amount is your strongest card. Bank records beat memory. Save the screenshots now.

### ✅ What to do next

1. Put **10 September 2028** in your phone calendar now. That is your hard deadline.
2. Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in. They take it up with the company directly.
3. Save your evidence somewhere it cannot be lost: screenshot the chats, download the bank statement, photograph the agreement.
4. If that fails: District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
