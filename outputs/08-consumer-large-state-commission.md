## Where you stand — ₹75,00,000

### ⏳ Your deadline

🟢 **You have about 531 days left.**

You have **2 years** from **20 April 2026** (the date the problem arose or the refund was refused). That runs out on **20 April 2028**.

### 🏛️ Where to go

**Try these first — they are free and often enough:**

- **National Consumer Helpline (1915)** — Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in. They take it up with the company directly.
  - Usually takes: days to a few weeks

**If that does not work:**

- **State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission** — fee ₹2,000; longer than the District Commission; you can do this yourself
  - File at the State Commission for your state, or online at edaakhil.nic.in.

### 📄 What to gather

**You already have:**

- ✅ proof that you paid (bank entry, receipt or bill)

Anything in writing where they admit the amount is your strongest card. Bank records beat memory. Save the screenshots now.

### ✅ What to do next

1. Put **20 April 2028** in your phone calendar now. That is your hard deadline.
2. Call 1915 or file at consumerhelpline.gov.in. They take it up with the company directly.
3. Save your evidence somewhere it cannot be lost: screenshot the chats, download the bank statement, photograph the agreement.
4. If that fails: State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

### 🚨 You should speak to a real lawyer

- 🟡 The amount here is large enough that a lawyer's fee is small by comparison.

Free legal aid: call **15100** (NALSA), or walk into the District Legal Services Authority at your district court. Both are free.
