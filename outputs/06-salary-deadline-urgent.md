## Where you stand — ₹14,000

### ⏳ Your deadline

🟠 **You have about 29 days left.**

You have **12 months** from **05 December 2025** (the date the first unpaid salary became payable). That runs out on **05 December 2026**.

⚠️ That is soon. Treat this as urgent.

### 🏛️ Where to go

**Try these first — they are free and often enough:**

- **Written demand to the employer** — Email HR and the reporting manager with the exact amount and period, and ask for a written reason if they refuse. Keep the thread.
  - Usually takes: 15 to 30 days

**If that does not work:**

- **Authority under the Payment of Wages Act** — No court fee for the application; varies by state, often a few months; you can do this yourself
  - Apply to the Authority appointed under the Payment of Wages Act for your area, through the state Labour Department or Labour Commissioner's office.
- **Labour Commissioner / conciliation** — Free; weeks to months; you can do this yourself
  - File a complaint with the office of the Labour Commissioner for your district. They call both sides for conciliation.
- **Civil suit for recovery** — Court fee on a percentage of the amount claimed, varying by state; a year or more; lawyer recommended
  - A recovery suit in the civil court with jurisdiction. Usually the last resort for salary.

### 📄 What to gather

**Worth trying to get:**

- ⬜ the appointment letter or contract

Anything in writing where they admit the amount is your strongest card. Bank records beat memory. Save the screenshots now.

_Missing a document does not sink your case. A written message where the other side admits the amount is often worth more than a formal receipt, so look through your WhatsApp and email first._

### ✅ What to do next

1. Put **05 December 2026** in your phone calendar now. That is your hard deadline.
2. Email HR and the reporting manager with the exact amount and period, and ask for a written reason if they refuse. Keep the thread.
3. Save your evidence somewhere it cannot be lost: screenshot the chats, download the bank statement, photograph the agreement.
4. If there is no reply in 15 days, send a written legal notice. This tool can draft one for you from the facts above.
5. If that fails: Authority under the Payment of Wages Act.
